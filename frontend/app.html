Who we have paid

our record, written at settlement — not the seller's

A supplier can inflate its own star rating; it cannot make us have paid it. A tally is not a reputation — anything with concerns sorts first, because the biggest number must not bury the row worth reading.

The fleet

a second bound, and one that fails differently

Attenuation stops a token being widened. The registry stops one being minted wider than the agent was ever approved to hold. Two checks that fail for different reasons, so a bug in one does not disable the other.

Standing policies

recurring spend, inert until a human approves it

Persistence changes the shape of an injection. One against an errand buys the wrong thing once; one that lands while a recurring policy is being written keeps paying out for months — and the mechanism that makes ticks cheap (pure arithmetic, no model) is exactly what stops anything noticing later. So a policy does nothing until approved.

The ceiling is cryptographic, not a setting — it lives in the token, and no agent below can raise it.
